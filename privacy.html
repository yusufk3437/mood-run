Privacy Notice

Version: October 24, 2026

This notice describes data handled by the current browser version of Mood Run.

Data saved on your device

The game uses browser local storage for your player name, gold and gem balances, owned and selected skins, best score, daily reward progress, sound and vibration preferences, and the version of terms you accepted. Older versions may also have saved a language preference. These records are not an online account.

You can remove this information using your browser's settings to clear site data. This also resets your local progress and causes the game to ask for acceptance again. If storage is unavailable, progress or acceptance may not persist between visits.

Multiplayer connections

When you choose multiplayer, PeerJS signaling helps connect the two players. Your player name, selected skin, player movement, round information, and game status are exchanged with the other player's browser. Connection services and peers may receive network information, including IP addresses, as part of establishing the connection.

Use a nickname rather than sensitive personal information. A player who has your room code may attempt to join. Room codes and connections are not a private messaging or secure identity system.

Hosting and external resources

Your browser requests the game from its hosting provider. It also loads Three.js from cdnjs, PeerJS from unpkg, and fonts from Google Fonts. Multiplayer contacts PeerJS connection services. These providers receive ordinary request and network information, such as IP address, browser details, and requested resources, under their own policies. Hosting and connection providers may retain access logs.

Ads, payments, and analytics

The current game's rewarded-ad screens are simulations. No real ad SDK, payment processor, or analytics SDK is integrated into this version. Local progress is not uploaded by an account or cloud-save feature. This notice must be reviewed if those features are added.

Your choices

You can play single player without opening a multiplayer room, turn off sound and vibration in Settings, clear site data to delete local records, or close the game. Closing the game ends its active peer connection.

See the Terms of Service for conditions of play.
